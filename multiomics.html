Curation and reuse notes

The catalog distinguishes raw spectra, processed public tables and publication-only evidence so users can select data appropriate for reanalysis.

Scope and quality control

    Database methods statement

    Publicly available Liriodendron proteomics and metabolomics resources were manually curated from MassIVE/GNPS and article-associated repositories. Records were standardized by species, tissue, analytical platform, data level and accession. For mixed-species MassIVE projects, only Liriodendron-labelled spectra and the corresponding blank, pooled quality-control and identification files were retained in the file-level index.
